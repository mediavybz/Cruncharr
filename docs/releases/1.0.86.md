**New**

- Schedule future episodes of a series, including later seasons, with pause and resume controls.
- Register Premium downloads in Sonarr and submit completed files for import. Guest and free accounts can use **Request in Sonarr** to search through their configured Sonarr sources.
- See Sonarr library badges while browsing and searching, and hide series already in Sonarr.

**Fixed**

- Browse and search without signing in. Cached results appear as you type, and Browse includes catalog entries with no episodes currently listed.
- Recognize more alternate series titles in Sonarr and improve episode matching for specials, recaps, and alternate editions. Keep verified library matches after restarts and connection failures.
- Keep browsing position during background updates and load current UI files after an app update.
- Show Sonarr file counts correctly in History and restore saved dub and subtitle overrides.
- Save settings reliably and apply changes to scheduling, queue limits, proxies, backgrounds, and download overrides.
- Apply transcoding profiles correctly, preserve extra audio and subtitle tracks, and report encoding failures without discarding the source file.

**Removed**

- Inactive API-selection, default-search-mode, and MITM settings controls.

**Docker:** `ghcr.io/mediavybz/cruncharr:latest` or `ghcr.io/mediavybz/cruncharr:1.0.86` (AMD64 and ARM64). Update the container, then refresh the app page.
